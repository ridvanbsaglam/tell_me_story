🎴 Tell Me!

An interactive storytelling card game powered 100% natively by Rive & Luau

🎨 72 Watercolor Assets ⚡ 60 FPS Rive Canvas ⌨️ Physical & Touch Typing 📐 Adaptive Full-Bleed 🚀 Zero Heavyweight LMS

Level 1: Rookie

Untimed, relaxed storytelling. Flip cards and mark them done as you invent your tale.

Level 2: Expert

Beat the 30-second timer! Tests quick thinking and rapid improv creativity.

Level 3: Master

Introduces a sealed envelope with a mystery twist sentence to incorporate.

Level 4: Guru

Full writing studio! Use your physical keyboard or quick chips, then export your story card.
